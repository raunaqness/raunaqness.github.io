**[5/5] My first dream car** - Abhijit Latkar (ZX Petrol Manual)

I purchased the new Honda Amaze about a month ago, and after driving it for around 1500 km in mixed conditions, here’s my detailed experience:
 Looks & Style – 4.8/5
The Amaze stands out with its clean and elegant styling. The front looks premium, and the overall design feels modern and well-balanced.
Reason for slight deduction: the rear side lacks some lighting elements, which makes it look a bit plain compared to the front. A slightly enhanced tail-light setup would have made it perfect.


Mileage – 4.0/5
Mileage before the first service has been average but acceptable:

Highway: 16–17 kmpl

City: 8–12 kmpl
Considering it's still before the first major service, there's room for improvement, but it performs decently on long drives.

 Safety – 4.7/5

The Amaze feels secure on the road. The ADAS functions work perfectly, especially in traffic and on highways. It adds a solid confidence boost for daily commuting and weekend trips.

 Power – 4.2/5

Powered by the i-VTEC engine, the car delivers sufficient and smooth power for city traffic and occasional highway runs. It’s not extremely sporty, but it never feels underpowered.
 Comfort – 4.5/5

Ride comfort in the city is impressive with a well-tuned suspension.
However, on highways, the car feels slightly bumpy at higher speeds or on uneven patches.

 Interiors – 4.9/5

The cabin stands out with simple, clean, and classy interiors. The layout is user-friendly, the materials feel good, and the overall ambience is elegant. Hard to find anything to complain about here.

Overall Impression

The Honda Amaze 3G is a stylish, comfortable, and safe compact sedan that performs well for daily use. With refined interiors and reliable Honda engineering, it’s a great buy for those looking for a practical and premium-feeling car.
